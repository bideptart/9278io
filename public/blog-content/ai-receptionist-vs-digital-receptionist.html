What happens when a customer calls after hours?

Most Indian businesses lose calls quietly. A missed call at 9pm does not show up as an angry review; the caller simply rings the next name on their list and never mentions why they left. Front desks built for daytime hours were never designed to catch what happens after the shutters close.

  • A caller who reaches a dead line after hours rarely tries again the next morning.
  • Weekend enquiries pile up unanswered until Monday, by which point most have already booked elsewhere.
  • A single missed call rarely feels urgent, but a year of them adds up to real lost revenue.
  • Businesses that track this properly are often surprised by how many calls land outside working hours.
