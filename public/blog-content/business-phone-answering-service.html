Human-staffed or AI-powered? Two very different answering models

A traditional business phone answering service routes your calls to a shared team of live agents, usually working fixed shifts from a call centre. An AI-powered version answers every call itself, using an AI voice agent instead of a rostered team. Both aim to catch business calls you would otherwise miss, but they scale and cost very differently.

What a human-staffed service offers

A live agent can handle nuance and empathy well, but is limited to one call at a time and a fixed shift schedule.

What an AI-powered service offers

An AI voice agent answers instantly, handles many business calls at once, and never needs a shift handover.

Five questions to ask before you sign an answering service contract

Sales pages rarely mention the details that decide whether a business phone answering service actually fits your call volume. Asking these questions upfront avoids a contract that looks cheap on paper and expensive once real usage shows up.

  1. Is every call billed per second, or rounded up to the next full minute?
  2. What happens to your business calls once you exceed the plan's included minutes?
  3. Is there a long-term contract, or can you scale usage up and down freely?
  4. Does the service support the Indian languages your actual callers use?
  5. Can it handle a sudden spike in business calls without dropping any of them?
