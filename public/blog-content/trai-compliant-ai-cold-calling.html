AI Voice Agent Compliance in India: Staying TRAI and DPDP Compliant in 2026

By 9278.io Team · 8 June 2026 · 9 min read

An AI voice agent on your phones still has to follow India's telecom and data rules, or your lines can get disconnected. Here's what TRAI and the DPDP Act actually require in 2026, in plain English.

9

Introduction

An AI voice agent can handle a huge share of your calls, but India's telecom and data rules are strict and the penalties are real. This guide explains, in plain English, exactly what you need to stay compliant, whether your agent is answering inbound calls or your business also sends any commercial communication.

The core rulebook: TCCCPR 2018

Let's be direct: AI-powered calling is legal in India, but only if you follow the rules, and in 2026 those rules have real teeth. All commercial calling falls under the Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TCCCPR), 2018, enforced by TRAI. The crucial 2025 update: AI-generated voices are now formally classified as "artificial" voices, so your AI agent is held to the same robocall rules as any automated dialling system. There's no loophole because "it's AI". This is a plain-English guide, not legal advice.

Register on DLT (this is non-negotiable)

Before you make commercial calls, you must be registered on the DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) platform of a telecom operator. Your business registers as a Principal Entity (PE), your calling provider registers as a telemarketer (RTM), and you complete a PE-TM binding linking the two, so every call is legally attributable. Without this, telcos are directed to block your commercial traffic. As of the February 2025 amendments, new registrations also require physical verification and Aadhaar-based biometric/OTP authentication.

Scrub against DND, and get consent right

Before dialling any list, scrub it against the National Customer Preference Register (the DND registry), every campaign, not once. The 2025 amendments also tightened consent: inferred consent (from an existing relationship) is valid only for the duration of that relationship; explicit consent to fulfil a specific transaction is valid for just 7 days; and a call only counts as transactional if it's triggered within 30 minutes of a customer action, otherwise it's promotional and needs promotional consent. Keep consent records and an audit trail.

Use the right number series and window

Route each type of traffic on the correct series: 140-series numbers are for promotional/marketing calls, and 160-series numbers are for transactional/service calls. Promotional calls are restricted to 9am-9pm. Mixing them is a violation. Also tell callers the conversation is recorded, store recordings securely, and process opt-out requests immediately; customers can now report spam within 7 days, so a clean opt-out process protects you.

Penalties, and the DPDP Act

The penalties are not trivial: first-time offenders can face a 15-day suspension of outgoing telecom services; repeat offenders can be disconnected from all telecom resources for a year and blacklisted. On top of TRAI's telecom rules, the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, with Rules notified in November 2025, governs the personal data behind your calls. You'll need lawful consent, security safeguards, a grievance officer, breach reporting, and data localisation for sensitive data. Full compliance is required by May 2027, but you should be building toward it now.

Conclusion

In conclusion, AI voice agents are both legal and powerful in India, but only with the right registrations, DND scrubbing, valid consent, and DPDP-ready data handling in place.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI calling legal in India in 2026?

Yes, but only if you comply with TRAI's TCCCPR rules. AI voices are classified as artificial voices, so commercial calling needs DLT/telemarketer registration, DND scrubbing, valid consent, correct number series, the 9am-9pm promotional window, and recording disclosure. Genuine inbound service calls carry a lighter burden.

What is DLT registration and do I need it for AI calling?

DLT registration is mandatory registration on a telecom operator's platform before making commercial calls. Your business registers as a Principal Entity, your provider as a telemarketer, and you complete a PE-TM binding. Without it, carriers block your commercial traffic.

How long is consent valid under the 2025 TRAI rules?

Inferred consent lasts only for the duration of the customer relationship, and explicit consent to fulfil a transaction is valid for just 7 days. A call is only transactional if triggered within 30 minutes of a customer action; otherwise it needs promotional consent.

What are the penalties for non-compliant AI calling in India?

First-time offenders can face a 15-day suspension of outgoing telecom services; repeat offenders can be disconnected from all telecom resources for up to a year and blacklisted. DPDP breaches carry separate, much larger financial penalties.

Does the DPDP Act apply to AI calling?

Yes. The DPDP Act 2023, with Rules notified in November 2025, governs the personal data behind your calls, requiring lawful consent, security safeguards, a grievance officer, breach reporting, and data localisation. Full compliance is required by May 2027.
