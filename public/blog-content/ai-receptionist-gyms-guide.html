What a membership freeze reminder call can and can't say

Renewal and freeze reminder calls are useful, and any AI receptionist for gyms sending them still has to follow TRAI's rules on timing and consent. A gym calling members by hand already avoids odd hours instinctively; an automated system needs that built in.

  • Reminder calls should only go out within TRAI's permitted calling windows.
  • Members should be able to opt out of reminder calls just as easily as with a staff call.
  • Consent for renewal and freeze reminders should be captured once and respected consistently.
