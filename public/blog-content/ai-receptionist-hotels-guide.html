Does a budget hotel need the same setup as a resort?

A budget hotel near a transit hub and a resort booked for a weekend getaway get very different calls, and treating them the same wastes the setup. AI receptionist for hotels should be configured around which one it is actually answering for.

What a budget hotel line mostly needs

What a resort line mostly needs

Detailed amenity questions, package inquiries, and reservation confirmations booked weeks in advance.

What a room reservation confirmation call should always include

A room reservation confirmation call is only useful if it captures what the front desk actually needs. Room reservations booked by an AI receptionist for hotels should carry the same detail a front desk agent would write down by hand.

  • Room type, check-in and check-out dates, and the agreed rate.
  • A callback number in case the booking needs to change.
  • Any special request, like an early check-in or an extra bed.
  • Confirmation sent back to the guest before the call ends.
